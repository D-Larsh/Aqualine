What We Offer

Aqualine is a simple technology platform that helps people report blocked drains before they cause serious flooding.

People can report a blocked drain using:

📱 A mobile app

💬 WhatsApp

📞 USSD or SMS

They can send a photo and location of the blocked drain. The information is then shown on a map so that the people responsible for cleaning the drains can quickly see where help is needed.

Why Choose Us

Aqualine is a low-cost, phone based solution which specifically targets blocked drains and flooding in Lagos.

Aqualine plans to work directly with The Lagos State Environmental Protection Agency (LASEPA), an agency responsible for protecting and improving the environment in Lagos State.
